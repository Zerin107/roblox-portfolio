Destruction Physics

This system works by carving out a hole in any rectangular part on the backend, and then on the frontend shooting out parts from the hole. The parts shot from the hole match the hole size.

It was created for a game where you blow up Brainrot characters, hence the use of brainrot characters in the clip. (unreleased game)
